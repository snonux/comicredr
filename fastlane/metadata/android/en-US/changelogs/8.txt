* Move marked comics to another folder (gm, or Move): pick it from a list you type into, or make a new one.
* gc puts the selected or marked comics in a collection.
* G12 goes to page 12; G alone to the last page.
* Parts of a page by two digits: 11-12 halves, 21-23 thirds, 31-34 strips, 41-44 quarters.
* The page shown whole before it turns when reading in parts.
